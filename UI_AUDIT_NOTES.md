# UI Audit — Hackathon Polish Pass

## What is already working

The deep-green, parchment, and muted-gold identity is calm and credible for a banking-risk demonstration. The current transfer intake is especially strong on mobile: the six scenarios precede the form, the form fields are comfortably touch-sized, and the primary action is obvious. The live-analysis panel also makes the rule-engine flow feel concrete.

## High-impact improvements selected

| Priority | Finding | Improvement | Why it matters in a jury demo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empty decision, investigation, and case screens are visually sparse. | Replace generic empty states with an audit-led start panel showing the next three steps and a prominent demo entry. | The product still feels intentional before the first scenario is run. |
| 2 | Product naming alternates between Sentinel and SentinelAI. | Standardise the visible product wordmark and evidence labels on **SentinelAI**. | Produces one memorable product identity. |
| 3 | A public restart path is not clearly exposed for a new jury walkthrough. | Add a non-destructive **New demo session** control that clears browser-only session outcomes with confirmation. | Lets a presenter reset quickly without touching stored or external data. |
| 4 | Regulatory and decision cues are present but not visually joined on initial states. | Use numbered policy rails, snapshot labels, and muted-gold authority cues in the redesigned empty states. | Strengthens the "audit-grade decision infrastructure" feeling without decorative clutter. |

## Non-changes by design

No real data, real banking integration, additional prediction model, or persistence is needed for this hackathon scope. These would add risk without improving the core demonstration.
